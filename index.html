Before you run

Required gear and reminders

  • Headlamp: required for the 21K, recommended for the 12K
  • Bring your own water bottle, hydration pack or foldable cup
  • Carry a fully charged phone with load, plus some extra cash
  • Race bib required. No bib, no run
  • Sign the race waiver before the gunstart
  • Missing required gear may result in a time penalty if podium results are disputed

Run with purpose

Bring your own tumbler, bottle or cup.

There are no disposable cups at any aid station.

Special awards

  • Top 3 male and female finishers in each category
  • Biggest delegation
  • Oldest runner
  • Youngest runner
